# The 7 Connector Buckets

Every business uses tools that fall into one of 7 categories. Claude connects to these through Cowork connectors. The goal of Phase 5 is to map what the user actually uses in each bucket — then enable the two must-haves (Calendar + Comms/Gmail) and note which other buckets are empty so the user can fill them over time.

---

## Bucket 1 — Revenue
**What it does:** Tracks money in. Invoicing, payment processing, financial dashboards, sales reporting.

**Common tools:** Stripe, GoHighLevel (payments), QuickBooks, FreshBooks, Wave, PayPal Business, HoneyBook, Dubsado, Xero.

**What Claude unlocks when connected:** Morning brief can surface overdue invoices and recent payments. Voice-writer can draft follow-up emails for unpaid invoices in the user's voice.

**Example question to ask:**
> "Bucket 1 — Revenue: where money comes in and where you check how the business is doing. Things like Stripe, GoHighLevel payments, QuickBooks. What do you use for this?"

---

## Bucket 2 — Customer
**What it does:** Stores and manages relationships. CRM, contact database, lead tracking, deal pipeline.

**Common tools:** GoHighLevel CRM, HubSpot, Salesforce, Notion CRM, Airtable CRM, Pipedrive, Close.io, ActiveCampaign.

**What Claude unlocks when connected:** Morning brief can surface leads that haven't been followed up in X days. Level-up and voice-writer can personalize outreach using real contact data.

**Example question to ask:**
> "Bucket 2 — Customer: where you track your clients and leads. Like GoHighLevel CRM, HubSpot, or a spreadsheet. What do you use?"

---

## Bucket 3 — Calendar
**What it does:** Manages time. Scheduled calls, reminders, recurring events, availability.

**Common tools:** Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, Calendly (read-only), Apple Calendar.

**What Claude unlocks when connected:** Morning brief shows today's schedule with prep notes. Onboarding Phase 5 enables Google Calendar with safe read/write permissions.

**Note:** This is one of the two must-have connectors. Walk the user through enabling it in this phase.

**Example question to ask:**
> "Bucket 3 — Calendar: where your schedule lives. Google Calendar, Outlook, anything you use to manage your time. What do you use?"

---

## Bucket 4 — Comms
**What it does:** Email and messaging. Where client conversations and team communications happen.

**Common tools:** Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams, Telegram, WhatsApp Business.

**What Claude unlocks when connected:** Morning brief surfaces urgent emails and team messages. Voice-writer drafts replies in the user's voice. Brief-source-comms pulls Slack/Discord/Teams messages.

**Note:** Gmail is the other must-have connector. Walk the user through enabling it with safe permissions in this phase (reads: act, send/delete: blocked or ask).

**Example question to ask:**
> "Bucket 4 — Comms: where you talk to clients and your team. Gmail, Slack, Discord, Teams — what do you use?"

---

## Bucket 5 — Tasks
**What it does:** Tracks work in progress. To-do lists, project boards, task assignments, deadlines.

**Common tools:** ClickUp, Notion (tasks), Asana, Trello, Monday.com, Linear, Todoist, Things 3.

**What Claude unlocks when connected:** Morning brief gains a Tasks section pulling overdue and due-today items. Tune-up can identify blocked tasks and suggest next moves.

**Example question to ask:**
> "Bucket 5 — Tasks: where you track what needs to get done. ClickUp, Notion, Asana, Trello — anything you use to manage your work. What do you use?"

---

## Bucket 6 — Meetings
**What it does:** Records, transcribes, and summarizes calls. Video conferencing and meeting intelligence.

**Common tools:** Zoom, Google Meet, Fireflies.ai, Otter.ai, Fathom, Loom, Microsoft Teams (meetings), Gong.

**What Claude unlocks when connected:** Voice-writer can pull yesterday's meeting transcript and draft accurate follow-up emails. Morning brief can include action items from yesterday's calls.

**Example question to ask:**
> "Bucket 6 — Meetings: where you run and record your calls. Zoom, Google Meet, Fireflies for transcripts — what do you use?"

---

## Bucket 7 — Knowledge
**What it does:** Stores reference material, SOPs, documentation, and shared team knowledge.

**Common tools:** Notion (pages/database), Google Drive, Dropbox, Confluence, Obsidian (local), Coda.

**What Claude unlocks when connected:** Voice-writer can reference SOPs when drafting content. Claude can surface relevant internal docs when answering questions about processes or offers.

**Example question to ask:**
> "Bucket 7 — Knowledge: where you keep SOPs, reference docs, and shared knowledge. Notion, Google Drive, Confluence — what do you use?"

---

## Quick reference table

| # | Bucket | What it does | Must-have? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue | Money tracking, invoicing | No — but unlock later |
| 2 | Customer | CRM, leads, relationships | No — but unlock later |
| 3 | Calendar | Schedule, events | **Yes — enable in Phase 5** |
| 4 | Comms | Email + team messaging | **Yes — Gmail in Phase 5** |
| 5 | Tasks | To-dos, project boards | No — but unlock for brief |
| 6 | Meetings | Call recording + transcripts | No — but unlock for voice-writer |
| 7 | Knowledge | SOPs, docs, reference | No — but unlock for context |

Two must-haves to enable today: **Calendar (Bucket 3)** and **Gmail (Bucket 4)**.
All others get recommended based on the user's business-brain.md and connections.md gaps.
